# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community. Everyone who participates in this project is expected to treat others with professionalism and respect.

## Expected Behavior

Please:

* Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
* Welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
* Give and receive feedback professionally.
* Assume good intentions and communicate in good faith.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not tolerated:

* Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
* Trolling, intimidation, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Sexualized or offensive language or imagery.
* Sharing private information without permission.
* Any other behavior that is inappropriate or harmful to the community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content, issue warnings, or restrict participation when this Code of Conduct is violated.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers privately. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful community.
